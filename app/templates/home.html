Things students should know earlier

  1. The program is genuinely physics-heavy.
  2. MRSc and MedBioPhys are not interchangeable.
  3. Research experience can change your entire path.
  4. Python and data skills compound quickly.
  5. Clinical medical physics requires graduate training after undergrad.
