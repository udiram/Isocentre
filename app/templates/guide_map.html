How to use this page

Read the card for your current stage first. Then scan the next two terms only. Save the rest for when it becomes relevant.

01 Choose the right program

Confirm that you want physics-first training, not MRSc-style direct clinical professional training.

02 Protect admission

Keep calculus, physics, chemistry, and biology buckets intact before choosing nice-to-have electives.

03 Survive the core

Level II is where study systems, lab habits, and coding practice start to matter every week.

04 Build evidence

Use research, co-op, thesis, projects, or portfolios to prove what kind of physicist you are becoming.

05 Choose the next credential

Clinical physics, health physics, imaging AI, biophysics, industry, and grad school have different next steps.
