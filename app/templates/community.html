Networking playbook

How to get a response worth reading

Ask one decision at a time

“Should I prioritize NSERC USRA or co-op this winter?” is easier to answer than “tell me about research.”

Give your context

Mention your stage, courses taken, interests, and what you already tried. Settings now fills some of this in for upper-year questions.

Use mentors for lived experience

Ask about workflow, preparation, timelines, and tradeoffs. Confirm official requirements with advisors or official McMaster pages.

Close the loop

If advice helped, leave a course review or join the mentor directory later. The hub gets better when people add practical details.
